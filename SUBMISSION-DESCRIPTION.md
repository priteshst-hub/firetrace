# FireTrace submission description

FireTrace connects NASA FIRMS satellite observations to transparent human review decisions. Its global explorer compares archived MODIS and VIIRS records from 2019–2020. FireTrace Command follows a Black Summer grid cell from source evidence through an explainable review category, human verification workflow and downloadable decision brief.

The replay uses 5,363 monthly 10 km grid records for southeastern Australia and an 82-row dated extract reconstructed from verified research inputs. Users can inspect platform counts, acquisition minutes and product confidence where available. The comparison identifies shared monthly activity and differences in recorded evidence while disclosing observation-window and coverage uncertainty.

Review priorities are unvalidated demonstration rules. The archive cannot establish present conditions, long-term trends, incident continuity, notification latency or operational accuracy. Ground reports, weather, exposure and incident status require independent verification. Current near-real-time access requires a server-side integration and authorized credentials.

Public source and reproducibility instructions are available at https://github.com/priteshst-hub/firetrace.
